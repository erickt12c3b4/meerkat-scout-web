What Scout does for Snapchat

03

Keeps availability accurate so ads don't promote products you've sold out of.

What Snapchat requires that others don't

  • An organization and an ad account — a Snapchat login alone can't own a catalog.
  • A public product URL per item, reachable by Snapchat's crawler.
  • Image dimensions suitable for vertical placements; Scout can select by rule.

Scout's starter template for Snapchat pre-maps these from Shopify where a mapping exists, and its diagnostics name the field when one is missing.
